# RIET Jaipur – Complete Website UI Prototype

## Preview
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
3. To review every screen from one place, open `page-index.html` (also linked as "All Pages" in navigation).
4. Every page is a separate `.html` file.

If you prefer a local server, open a terminal in this folder and run:
`python -m http.server 8000`
Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.

## Included pages
Home, About RIET, Leadership, Approvals & Affiliations, Academics, Departments,
Computer Science & Engineering (sample department page), Admissions, Fees & Scholarships,
Placements, Campus Life, Campus Facilities, Research & Innovation, Incubation Centre,
News & Events, Photo Gallery, Student Corner, Mandatory Disclosure and Contact.

## Notes
- Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype; no backend or CMS.
- Enquiry form is demo-only and does not send or store data.
- Photos are remote Unsplash placeholders; internet is needed to display them. Replace with college-owned approved photographs.
- Confirm current programmes, faculty, fees, approvals, policies, statistics and contact details with RIET before publishing.
- The RIET wordmark is a text placeholder; replace it with the official logo.
